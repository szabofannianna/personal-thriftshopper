# Finds page format

One child page of "Finds" per run. Title: `<Weekday d Mon> – N finds` (e.g. `Thu 15 Oct – 7 finds`). Use Notion markdown.

```
Prices include buyer protection, converted at 1 EUR = <rate> <site currency> (<source>, <date>). Colours are judged from photos, so they're estimates.

## <Wishlist item name>
### [<Listing title>](https://<site>/items/<id>)
![](<full signed photo URL>)
**≈ <price> <budget currency>** incl. buyer protection · <brand> · <size> · <material> · <condition>
Why: <one line — what matches; mark anything uncertain; name a specific favourite or an Active learning when it drove the pick, e.g. "same vibe as your oversized alpaca knit">

---

### Maybe
### [<Listing title>](https://<site>/items/<id>)
![](<photo>)
**≈ …** · …
Why: … (what's uncertain and why it's only a maybe)

---
```

Rules:
- One H2 per wishlist item that has finds; skip items with none.
- Strong matches first, then a "Maybe" H3 section for Balanced items.
- One photo per listing, the main photo. Never omit it.
- Keep "Why" to one line. No marketing language.
- End with: `Tick "Not for me" (with a reason) in Shown listings → Photos to teach the assistant what you don't want.`
