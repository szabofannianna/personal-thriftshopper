# Learning rules

## Signals

| Outcome | Meaning | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Hearted | Shown, then favourited on Vinted | strong yes (1) |
| Bought | Set by the user | strongest yes (2) |
| Not for me | Ticked by the user, ideally with a reason | strong no (1) |
| Ignored | Shown 14+ days ago, no reaction | weak no (0.5) |

Most users heart much of what they're shown, so a heart alone says little. The informative contrast is hearted vs not-for-me/ignored *within the same wishlist item*.

## What to look for

Compare Hearted/Bought against Not for me/Ignored along: silhouette and fit (oversized vs fitted, cropped vs long), colour shade, material and blend percentage, condition, era (vintage vs modern), brand and brand tier, price band, size labels, seller country, photo style (worn vs flat-lay), words in titles, and the wording of "Reason" notes. Also compare with Favourites for themes the user hearts outside the assistant's picks. Use the "Why it matches" text of Shown listings; don't revisit Vinted for this.

## Writing a learning

`<statement> — evidence: X of Y hearted vs Z of W rejected/ignored — confidence: low/medium/high — effect: <how ranking changes>`

- At least 3 supporting reactions before a learning is listed; under 6 it stays "low".
- Update or retire learnings when new evidence contradicts them; note the retirement in one line.
- Never re-add a learning under "Rejected by you" unless evidence is at least twice as strong, and then only as a suggestion.
- Learnings change ranking only. Anything that would change a hard rule (budget, size, material, exclusions, an item's existence) goes under "Suggestions for you", e.g.:
  - a budget that rejects most of what the user hearts,
  - a wishlist item with a hit rate under 20% after 10+ shown,
  - an exclusion that seems wrong,
  - a theme in 5+ new favourites that isn't on the wishlist.

- A learning needs at least 3 reactions behind it.
